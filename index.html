About CAMINTEL

Our Story

CAMINTEL Technology Solution LLC was created to help homeowners and businesses protect, improve, and regain control of their properties. After seeing the growing need for dependable fencing and security services in the Augusta area, Jonathan recognized that customers often had to hire separate companies for fences, security cameras, and gate automation. CAMINTEL was built to provide these services through one reliable point of contact.

Meet the Owner

Jonathan Bonilla

Founder & Project Lead

Jonathan brings a background in telecommunications engineering and a master’s degree in industrial project management to every CAMINTEL project. His technical knowledge and project management experience allow him to evaluate each property carefully, recommend practical solutions, and coordinate installations with attention to quality, functionality, and detail.
